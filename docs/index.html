Perturbation

After a converged solve, jitter g every frame and watch the re-solved contact race its analytic first-order prediction from the Jacobians.

base α– Δα (re-solved)– witness ‖actual − J·δξ‖– normal ∠(actual, n*+Jn·δξ)–

Twists δξ applied as g←g·Exp(δξ). Violet is the analytic prediction x*+J·δξ — not re-solved. It hugs the true markers while the Jacobian is meaningful and peels away once the pose turns degenerate. Sparkline: re-solved α vs α* + J·δξ.

shape A (solid) shape B (solid) α-scaled overlay witness point(s) contact normal

Solid bodies are the true pose; the gray wireframe is both shapes scaled by alpha until they just touch. When contact_manifold_dim > 0 every point of the returned ContactManifold is drawn — a shared line for a 1D contact, a spread for a 2D one — with the normal at each, since it's the same vector everywhere whenever normal_jacobian_valid.

During Perturb: faint markers hold the unperturbed solve, and the violet diamond & dashed arrow is the analytic prediction x*+J·δξ — it rides the true markers until the contact turns degenerate.
